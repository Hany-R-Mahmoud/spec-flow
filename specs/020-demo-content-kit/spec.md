# Feature Specification: Demo Content Kit

**Feature Branch**: `020-demo-content-kit`  
**Created**: 2026-05-15  
**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: `docs/manus/reports/raw/demo-artifact-pack.md`

## Goal

Convert accepted Manus demo material into repo-owned demo guidance that stays
aligned with real product behavior and avoids unsupported claims.

## Requirements

- **FR-001**: Keep approved demo scenarios for notifications, auth redesign,
  and analytics dashboard.
- **FR-002**: Keep a short demo flow that shows input, clarification, PRD,
  epics, stories, warnings, and export preview.
- **FR-003**: Claims must be labeled supported, unknown/verify, or do-not-claim.
- **FR-004**: Demo copy must not claim exact time savings, adoption, customer
  usage, or production reliability without evidence.

## Initial Artifact

- `docs/demo/specflow-demo-kit.md`

## Success Criteria

- Stakeholders can demo SpecFlow without relying on raw Manus output.
- Marketing/demo language remains factual.
