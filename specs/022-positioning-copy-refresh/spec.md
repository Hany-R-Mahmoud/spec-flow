# Feature Specification: Positioning Copy Refresh

**Feature Branch**: `022-positioning-copy-refresh`  
**Created**: 2026-05-15  
**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: `docs/manus/reports/raw/competitive-product-research.md`

## Goal

Refresh product positioning around SpecFlow AI's strongest current promise:
rough input to review-ready, export-ready delivery artifacts.

## Requirements

- **FR-001**: Emphasize structured review loop, quality warnings, and developer
  handoff.
- **FR-002**: Treat competitor research as strategy input, not proof.
- **FR-003**: Re-verify public competitor claims before landing-page or README
  comparisons.
- **FR-004**: Avoid claims about customer adoption, market share, or measurable
  productivity gains without evidence.

## Success Criteria

- Public copy is sharper but factual.
- Competitive claims are source-checked before publication.
